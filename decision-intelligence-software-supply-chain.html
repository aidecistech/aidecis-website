Decision intelligence software for supply chain: the 2026 shortlist

First, which problem

Three different jobs wear the same name.

Before comparing names, separate the jobs. Most "decision intelligence" tools do one of these well, and the one you need depends on where your decisions actually go wrong.

  • Make the planPlanning and optimisation suites (APS, IBP) decide what the plan should be: demand, supply, inventory, scenarios. The question they answer is "what is the best plan?"
  • Automate the routine decisionDecision-automation platforms recommend and execute the repetitive calls a planner would otherwise make. The question they answer is "can software take this decision for me?"
  • Govern the consequential decisionA decision-accountability layer governs the decisions that carry real consequence, across people, bots and agents: who may take it, what they weighed, what executed, what happened next. The question is "who is accountable, and can we prove it?"

Common questions

Decision intelligence for supply chain, in plain terms.

What is decision intelligence for supply chain?

It is software that helps an organisation make, and account for, the decisions that run a supply chain — not only forecast or plan, but decide and act. In practice the term covers three different jobs: making the plan, automating routine decisions, and governing the consequential ones. Knowing which job you need is the whole choice.

How is it different from supply-chain planning (APS/IBP)?

Planning tools answer "what should the plan be?" A decision-accountability layer like Aidecis answers "who was allowed to take this decision, what did they weigh, what executed, and what happened next?" You often want both: the plan from your planning suite, and the governance over the consequential decisions that follow.

Do I have to replace my ERP or planning tool?

Not with Aidecis. It is a layer that sits on top of SAP and the planning and execution systems you already run. It reads what happens, checks it against your signed authority, routes exceptions to the accountable owner, and records the outcome — without becoming your system of record.

Which is best for SAP process manufacturers in European industry?

For governing consequential decisions across people, bots and agents on SAP, with authority and an audit trail and EU data residency, that is the specific job Aidecis is built for. For large-scale planning or routine decision automation, a planning suite or a decision-automation platform may fit better, and can run alongside Aidecis.

Is Aidecis a manufacturing analytics or MES tool?

No. Aidecis does not measure equipment effectiveness, schedule the line or control machines. Process mining, MES and industrial AI do that well. Aidecis governs the consequential decisions that follow from them, across people, bots and agents, and links each one to its outcome.

Can Aidecis run next to Celonis, SAP Digital Manufacturing or Siemens Opcenter?

Yes, that is the setup it is designed for. Their findings and recommendations enter the decision chain as inputs or as registered actors, and approved changes reach their systems through the Aidecis gateway. Which connections apply is agreed per project.

Why trust a buyer's guide the vendor wrote?

You should read it critically. We have named real alternatives, described each fairly, and been explicit about where another tool fits better than we do. The honest test is your own decisions: if your problem is accountability across people and software, not producing a plan, the case for Aidecis should stand on its own.
