Europe, North America and Asia Pacific have a regional weight of 1. Latin America, the Middle East and Africa have a weight of 1.5. The first two regions are included. Above two regions, the regional fee uses the average weight of all selected regions, regardless of selection order. Additional sites receive a volume discount after site 50; each extra site adds only its own rate, without a price jump for the existing network. The subscription shown is the annual estimate divided by twelve; the contract is invoiced annually. The business-case panel below uses editable exposure, addressable-share and reduction assumptions.
