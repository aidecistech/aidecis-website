Assumptions behind value and payback

  • Value / annual costs compares the net value with the subscription plus one third of the implementation, written off over three years.

The value is an estimate calculated from your inputs, not a result, a forecast or a guarantee.

The estimate multiplies annual costs at risk by the addressable share and the expected reduction. The cost of trust is the review time per decision at the loaded hourly cost, plus compute, for every decision in scope; net value is the gross value minus that cost. Annual costs combine the subscription and one third of the implementation, written off over three years. Payback divides the one-time investment (implementation and pilot) by the monthly net value left after the subscription. Our business-case threshold is net value of at least 3× annual costs and payback within 24 months, at the upper end of every range. No competitor price is assumed.
