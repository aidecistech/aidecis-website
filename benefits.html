A numeric reference is available from Slimstock Core Platform: €36,000 per year for 100,000 SKUs, 10 users and one location, plus a €30,000 setup fee and a three-year minimum. Savings calculation, assumptions and sources

Each chosen slice uses the matching annual company KPI, a published benchmark where one exists and a modeled savings range of 5–15% (10% midpoint). The value-to-investment ratio compares midpoint savings with subscription plus one third of one-time implementation and pilot costs. Payback estimates the one-time costs against annual midpoint savings after subscription. Competitor and market benchmarks are cost context; none proves Aidecis-specific savings.

  • Expedite freight: annual freight spend × APQC’s 12% median expedited-cost share.
  • Inventory reallocation: average inventory value × APQC’s 10% median carrying-cost rate.
  • Supplier disruption and demand surge: annual revenue × 1.74% broad disruption exposure, from an FTI survey reported by APEC; allocated 60% / 40%.
  • PO exceptions, price variance, spot buys and policy exceptions: annual supply-chain spend × 1% sensitivity in the Aidecis cost overview; allocated 35% / 30% / 20% / 15%. This is arithmetic sensitivity, not a claimed market loss rate.
  • Express spares and breakdown response: user-entered annual downtime cost, allocated 60% / 40%.
  • Governed Knowledge: a 10% modeled uplift to savings from selected decision slices. No comparable standalone global financial benchmark was found.
  • 5–15% scenario range: broad supply-chain cost-reduction range in BCG’s 2024 analysis ↗. It is not a decision-specific or Aidecis result.

Inputs are already annual totals, so decision volume is not multiplied by these baselines. The profile values stay in this browser session and are not sent to Aidecis. Validate the relevant cost pool, attribution and outcomes with operational and finance owners before relying on a business case.
