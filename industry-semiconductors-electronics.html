Semiconductors and electronics

Semiconductor and electronics production spans many process steps, countries and specialised suppliers. Concentrated manufacturing and equipment capacity can turn a local disruption into a network-wide shortage. Allocation and alternative sourcing need an explicit trade-off.

Current challenges

  • Concentrated fabrication and equipment capacity creates shared points of failure.
  • Long international process chains amplify disruptions and geopolitical exposure.
  • Capacity allocation and component substitutions can change customer commitments.

How Aidecis helps

  • Connect allocation options with customer priority, approved-source evidence and authority.
  • Surface downstream effects before a local sourcing or scheduling change proceeds.
  • Track exceptions and outcomes across the covered supplier and production network.

Potential benefits

  • Clearer allocation decisions during shortages.
  • Faster escalation of costly supply recovery choices.
  • More consistent decisions across sites and customer programmes.

These are potential improvements to test with your team. We agree a baseline, measure the pilot results and avoid counting the same benefit twice.

Operating context

Customer specifications, qualified-source controls and applicable trade and export restrictions.
