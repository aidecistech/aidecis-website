Aerospace and defense

Long lead times, specialised suppliers and part traceability make aerospace and defense supply chains unforgiving. Production ramp-up and maintenance add pressure to scarce components. Changes must respect certification requirements, approved sources and applicable export restrictions.

Current challenges

  • Long lead times and scarce qualified components threaten production and maintenance schedules.
  • Substitutions must respect traceability, approved sources and applicable certification requirements.
  • Production ramp-up creates allocation conflicts across programmes and suppliers.

How Aidecis helps

  • Compare recovery options with their evidence, approval requirements and downstream effects.
  • Route component changes, urgent procurement and allocation exceptions to named owners.
  • Record the authorised action and execution response within the agreed security and hosting scope.

Potential benefits

  • Faster review of qualified recovery options.
  • Better visibility of supplier bottlenecks and conflicting priorities.
  • Less time reconstructing approvals for industrial and logistics exceptions.

These are potential improvements to test with your team. We agree a baseline, measure the pilot results and avoid counting the same benefit twice.

Operating context

AS9100 requirements where applicable; relevant national and EU export controls, and ITAR where US-controlled items or data are involved. Hosting and security acceptance are agreed per project.
