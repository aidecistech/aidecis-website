Machinery and industrial equipment

High-mix, low-volume and engineer-to-order production brings many unique parts, engineering changes and project commitments. Long service lives add spare-parts dependencies. Local choices need to remain connected to cost, specification and delivery consequences.

Current challenges

  • Unique parts and engineering changes make project commitments difficult to protect.
  • Long-lead components create competing priorities across orders.
  • Service and spare-parts emergencies can generate costly commitments.

How Aidecis helps

  • Connect the proposed change with engineering evidence, cost and customer acceptance.
  • Route project allocation and sourcing exceptions to the responsible owners.
  • Keep the approved instruction and its outcome visible across the covered network.

Potential benefits

  • Faster agreement on acceptable engineering and sourcing changes.
  • Better control of expedite costs and project-margin exposure.
  • A reusable record of recurring service and spare-parts exceptions.

These are potential improvements to test with your team. We agree a baseline, measure the pilot results and avoid counting the same benefit twice.

Operating context

Project specifications, customer acceptance and applicable product-safety requirements.
