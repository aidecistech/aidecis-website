The way in

The decision replay assessment

Four weeks, a fixed fee, read-only on your own SAP purchasing history. Aidecis replays six months of your decisions against your policy and your delegation of authority, and shows you where the chain runs without an owner.

Nothing is written to any of your systems. You end with the evidence to decide whether to run one decision in shadow, and what a gated write route would take.

What it delivers ↓

What it delivers

Four weeks, four answers.

Read-only on your own SAP purchasing extract, the assessment answers the questions a point solution cannot.

  • The funnel from change to case

    How many SAP purchasing changes in the last six months became consequential decisions, and how many of those crossed teams or systems.

  • Cases per approver per day

    The real load on the people who hold authority, so you can see where review time is the cost of trust.

  • Gaps in the authority matrix

    Decisions and writes that fell outside the signed delegation of authority, including your own algorithms and other vendors' agents.

  • Software that writes unregistered

    Agents and algorithms that write in SAP with their own credentials, against no owner, role or limit.

How the four weeks run

Fixed scope, fixed deliverables.

  • Week 0Scope agreed, the SAP extract requested, one decision type chosen with one hard measure in your own terms.
  • Weeks 1–2Your decisions replayed against your policy and authority, read-only, on the History replay page.
  • Week 3The funnel, the authority gaps and the unregistered writes, reviewed with your owners.
  • Week 4The report as a PDF, and the plan for a shadow run and one gated write route.

What you provide

One read-only SAP purchasing extract.

Purchasing documents and their history, goods receipts and change documents, with stock, production demand and invoice items where a chain or a price envelope is to be replayed. No connection to any live system is needed.

What follows

Shadow first, then one gated write.

The first slice is always read-only, and a shadow result is a valid outcome. Only after the assessment and a shadow run does one decision move to a single gated write route into your own SAP test system, under its own authority.
