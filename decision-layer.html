The decision layer

One door for every signal. One question for the person who decides.

The same problem reaches your company in four places at once: a change in SAP, an alert in o9, a supplier's email, a planner's WhatsApp. Aidecis reads them all, sees that they belong together, and gives the person with the authority one clear question.

Your systems keep doing what they do. Aidecis sits across them, for the decisions that have consequences.

See it happen ↓

The problem

Four channels, four people, one problem.

Each system sees only its own signals. SAP knows the delivery moved, o9 knows the plan breaks, the buyer has the supplier's email and the planner knows the line will stop. Nobody sees the whole case, so the decision is late, made twice, or made by someone without the authority.

  • Signals get lost

    An email or a chat message never reaches the person who decides, or reaches them after the deadline.

  • Decisions are made twice

    Two people act on the same problem from different signals, with different facts.

  • Authority is unclear

    Whoever sees the message first decides, not the person whose mandate covers it.

  • Nothing is traceable

    Afterwards nobody can show which facts were known, who decided and why.

How it works

From four signals to one decision, in minutes.

Every source comes in through the same door. Aidecis reads each signal, joins the ones about the same object, routes the case by your own authority rules, and asks one person one question.

  1. Every source, one doorSAP, o9, email, WhatsApp or your own agents. A new source is a small adapter, not a new project.
  2. S-CLAIM readsIt recognises the decision and its key figure. A figure the signal does not give is asked for, never guessed.
  3. Code joinsSignals about the same order or shipment become one case. A likely match is asked, never assumed.
  4. Your rules routeYour delegation of authority names who decides. Above a limit, a second person confirms.
  5. One person decidesOne question, the evidence one tap away. Nothing runs without that decision, and everything is recorded.

Why a layer

Your systems stay. Aidecis connects the decisions across them.

Aidecis does not replace SAP, o9 or your communication channels. It reads from them, read-only, and adds what none of them has on its own: the whole case, the authority, and the record.

  • Read-only by design

    Aidecis reads your systems and never writes into them. Your own system carries out an approved action.

  • Your authority, applied by code

    The model reads and explains. Who decides, and whether a case waits for a person, is set by code from your own rules.

  • Every signal kept with the decision

    The record shows what each system and each person reported, who decided, and what happened next.

  • Your data stays in your environment

    Hosted in the EU. Your data trains the Aidecis model only if you opt in, in writing.
