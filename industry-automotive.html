Automotive

Automotive supply chains connect many supplier tiers with just-in-time and just-in-sequence production, and EVs add battery materials and battery regulation. Recovery decisions are taken by planners, by your own MRP, call-off and sequencing logic, and more and more by the agents of suppliers, carriers and procurement vendors. Aidecis gives all of them one authority model and one decision record, so a late part or a substitution is handled within the same limits, whoever or whatever acts.

Current challenges

  • Late components can stop an assembly line.
  • EV battery sourcing adds raw-material and supplier qualification risks.
  • Urgent freight and local substitutions can affect other plants and customer commitments.

How Aidecis helps

  • Register the software that already decides, such as MRP runs, call-off and sequencing logic, supplier-portal agents and carrier booking agents, with an owner, limits and a mode, next to the planners and buyers who approve.
  • Enforced control: a recovery action sent through the Aidecis gateway, such as premium freight or a substitution, runs only with a single-use grant, after spend, quantity and approved-source limits are checked.
  • Observed control: when a supplier's or carrier's agent changes an order or a booking in its own system, Aidecis reads the change afterwards, checks it against that agent's role and limits, and flags it within minutes if it went outside them. It does not prevent the change.
  • Connect stock cover, approved sources and production commitments before a recovery decision, and follow its effect on other plants.
  • Automation can climb furthest in reversible, capped decisions such as standard expedites or call-off adjustments: up to bounded bot mode (autonomy level 3) for one decision type and plant, once the run history shows it is stable. Substitutions of safety-relevant parts or parts that need customer approval stay with people.
  • How far a decision may climb also depends on your maturity in that decision family. For basic or digital organisations we propose reviewed assistance and bounded pilots, not bot mode. Agent-to-agent chains are not offered at launch.

Potential benefits

  • Less avoidable line disruption and emergency transport.
  • Faster approval of qualified recovery options.
  • Faster response to a late part, measured in hours from signal to approved recovery.

These are potential improvements to test with your team. We agree a baseline, measure the pilot results and avoid counting the same benefit twice.

Operating context

EU Battery Regulation where applicable; customer specifications and approved-source requirements.
