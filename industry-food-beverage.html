Food and beverage

Perishable and seasonal materials meet volatile commodity prices, food-safety requirements and retailer commitments. A delivery change can affect shelf life, production, waste and service at the same time. Teams need to see the whole trade-off before committing.

Current challenges

  • Perishable materials lose value while teams wait for decisions.
  • Seasonal supply and commodity-price changes create costly trade-offs.
  • Ingredient substitutions, recalls and retailer commitments can affect several sites at once.

How Aidecis helps

  • Bring shelf life, stock, supplier approval and customer commitments into the decision.
  • Escalate costly recovery transport or substitutions beyond delegated limits.
  • Track authorised changes and recurring exceptions so teams can improve the process.

Potential benefits

  • Less avoidable waste and lost shelf life.
  • Better control of premium freight and substitution costs.
  • Faster, evidenced responses to service and food-safety exceptions.

These are potential improvements to test with your team. We agree a baseline, measure the pilot results and avoid counting the same benefit twice.

Operating context

Food safety and traceability requirements; FSMA for relevant US operations.
