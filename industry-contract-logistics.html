Contract logistics

3PL and e-commerce fulfilment operators coordinate multiple client networks, sites and service agreements. Rerouting, returns and customs exceptions move quickly. Each decision needs the right client rules, spending authority and accountable owner.

Current challenges

  • Many client networks have different approval rules and service commitments.
  • Rerouting, customs delays and returns require decisions across sites.
  • Extra handling and recovery transport can erode contract margins.

How Aidecis helps

  • Apply the relevant client rules and spending envelope to each proposed action.
  • Route cross-site and customs trade-offs to accountable owners.
  • Connect the approved instruction to its execution response and service outcome.

Potential benefits

  • Fewer unauthorised client costs and service exceptions.
  • Less coordination work across warehouses and transport teams.
  • A clearer decision record for client reviews and cost discussions.

These are potential improvements to test with your team. We agree a baseline, measure the pilot results and avoid counting the same benefit twice.

Operating context

Client service agreements, customs requirements and access boundaries between client networks.
