S-CLAIM

Meet S-CLAIM, the Supply Chain Learning AI Model.

S-CLAIM brings the knowledge of thousands of supply-chain decisions to your operation on day one. It recognises the decision behind a signal, warns before friction and explains who decides and why.

Your knowledge base makes Aidecis sharper on your operation every week. Your data never trains S-CLAIM.

How S-CLAIM is trained ↓

What S-CLAIM does

Four skills, one per step of a decision.

A signal arrives: an alert from the TMS, a change in SAP, an email from a supplier. S-CLAIM reads it the way an experienced planner would.

  • Recognises the decision

    It names which of 71 supply-chain decision types the signal starts, and the figure the decision turns on.

  • Warns before friction

    It judges how likely the decision is to escalate, and to be reopened later, before anyone has acted.

  • Knows what comes first

    Safety, rules, time or money: it says what the decision has to protect first.

  • Explains the route

    Who decides, under which limit, and why, in plain English, Dutch or German that a controller can check.

How S-CLAIM is trained

Trained on how supply-chain decisions work, not on anyone's data.

General AI models learn from whatever text they can find. S-CLAIM learns from a body of supply-chain decision knowledge that Aidecis built and owns, in five steps.

  • 1 · The decision library71 decision types in 12 families, from expediting freight to a product recall. For each type: the signal that starts it, the evidence it needs, who holds the authority and which limits apply.
  • 2 · Rules judged by practitionersFor every type, when a decision escalates, when it gets reopened and what it protects first is written down as a rule, and each rule is judged by Aidecis's supply-chain leadership. No rule goes in without that verdict.
  • 3 · Realistic casesFrom those rules come thousands of cases in English, Dutch and German, with values checked against public freight and procurement data. Who approves each case is worked out by the same policy engine that runs in Aidecis, so S-CLAIM learns the route your policy takes.
  • 4 · Knowing when to stopSome cases leave out a deciding fact, or carry one that is out of date or unconfirmed. There the right answer is to hold and ask for that fact. S-CLAIM is trained to say what it needs first, instead of guessing.
  • 5 · Tested before releaseEvery version sits a fixed exam of 300 cases that Aidecis's supply-chain leadership answered blind, and that S-CLAIM never sees in training. A version is released only when it beats the general model it started from.

Why it is different

Built for decisions with consequences.

  • Owned, not borrowed

    The decision library, the rules and the cases are Aidecis's own work, built from years of running supply chains.

  • Your data stays yours

    S-CLAIM is never trained on client content. Your knowledge base improves your own environment, and nothing else unless you explicitly opt in.

  • European by design

    S-CLAIM is built on an open-weight model and runs on EU-owned infrastructure. No third-party AI vendor is in the path.

  • It advises, your policy decides

    S-CLAIM never sets a limit or grants an approval. Authority comes from your own delegation-of-authority matrix in the Aidecis policy engine.
