The company behind Aidecis

Aidecis is the decision intelligence and control layer for consequential supply-chain decisions. It is an Angeleus SLU company, based in Andorra, and it is built for European industry.

This page names the company and the way we work with clients, so your procurement team and investors can see who they are dealing with.

How we work with clients

A product, deployed by people who know supply chains.

  • Decision engineers at your site

    Decision engineers work at the client's site. They bring supply-chain depth in logistics, planning, procurement or quality. With your team they map the decision chain, build the baseline and the evaluation set from your past cases, configure triggers, policies and connectors, and coach your decision owners.

  • Sign-off before anything runs

    A workflow runs beyond shadow only when the decision engineer and your decision owner have signed it off on its evaluation results. Every client starts with a paid pilot or evidence sprint on one decision type.

  • The library rule

    Every engagement returns at least one certified workflow to the Aidecis library, as method only, never as your data. The library grows with each client, so the engineering effort per client falls over time and Aidecis stays a product company, not a consultancy.

  • You own your governance

    You own your decisions, dossiers, run records, evaluation sets and accepted policies, and you can export them at any time. Aidecis owns its methods, agents and library.

European focus

Built for European operations.

European supply chains run across sites, languages, jurisdictions and local operating rules. Aidecis keeps local authority visible and hosts client data in the EU, on European-owned providers.
