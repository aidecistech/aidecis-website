Decision intelligence, applied where it matters

Aidecis gives every one of them the same authority model and one decision record, linked to the outcome. Logistics · reference dossier

Expedite freight after disruption

The decisionA late arrival puts a production line or a customer order at risk. A carrier quotes an expedite, and someone has to decide whether the premium is worth it.

Who takes partThe planner and the logistics manager; the carrier-booking rules in the TMS; more and more often, a carrier's or visibility platform's agent that proposes the expedite.

What Aidecis doesPrepares one brief with the options against the total cost of the disruption and the past outcomes at the site, and routes it to the person whose limit covers it. The TMS booking runs only with a single-use grant for exactly the approved booking (enforced control). The invoice is later checked against that grant.

How far it can goStarts at autonomy level 1: agents prepare, a person decides. Small, reversible expedites under a tight cap can move to level 2 or 3 once the record shows they hold.

The measurePremium freight spend per month against the baseline. Also: line-stoppage hours avoided, time to decision, invoice variance.

Decision map · reference case

Expedite of a late inbound shipment, step by step.

From signal to verified outcome, with the registered actor and the system connection at each step. Illustrative example: names, limits and amounts are set with each client.

  1. Signal

    The carrier's ETA feed moves an inbound shipment two days late, past the material cut-off for a production line.

    Actor
    The carrier's visibility platform
    System
    Carrier ETA feed, read only

  2. Context

    Aidecis links the shipment to the production order, stock cover and line plan, prices the options (wait, partial air, full expedite) and adds past expedites at the site.

    Actor
    Aidecis prepares the brief; it does not decide
    System
    SAP and the TMS, read only

  3. Applicable policy

    The delegation-of-authority policy sets who may approve an expedite of this size, the cap per shipment and the approved carriers.

    Actor
    Policy set by the client's supply-chain director
    System
    Client policy and limits held in Aidecis

  4. Accountable owner

    The brief goes to the named logistics manager whose limit covers the premium. The approval, the option chosen and the reason are recorded.

    Actor
    Named logistics manager
    System
    Aidecis decision record

  5. Scoped action

    Enforced: the approved booking goes to the TMS through the Aidecis gateway with a single-use grant for exactly that carrier, shipment and amount. Observed: a carrier's agent books in its own system, and Aidecis reads the booking back afterwards and flags any difference; it does not block it.

    Actor
    Aidecis gateway, or the carrier's registered booking agent
    System
    TMS booking interface

  6. Verified outcome

    Aidecis reads back the actual arrival, the goods receipt and the carrier invoice, and compares them with the approved grant and the baseline.

    Actor
    Logistics manager; finance for any invoice variance
    System
    TMS and SAP, read back

Inventory · allocation

Inventory reallocation

The decisionStock runs short at one site while another holds a surplus. Moving it costs transport and may leave the sending site short.

Who takes partSite planners and the supply planner; replenishment rules and the ERP's transfer proposals; a planning vendor's optimiser.

What Aidecis doesRanks the receiving sites by service risk, protects minimum stock and reserved orders at the sending site, and creates the transfer in the WMS or ERP only after approval (enforced control). A transfer the optimiser books on its own is read back, checked against its limit and flagged when it goes over (observed control).

How far it can goLevel 2 is realistic early: the optimiser proposes, a planner approves above a set quantity.

The measureStockout hours at the receiving site. Also: fill rate, transfer cost, policy exceptions.

Planning · allocation

Demand surge allocation

The decisionA demand spike or a large order exceeds the supply available to promise, and someone decides which customers get what.

Who takes partDemand and supply planners, and the commercial owners; the allocation engine in the planning system.

What Aidecis doesCompares allocation options on customer commitments, margin, strategic rules and fairness, records who approved each change to a customer promise, and updates planning or the ERP only after approval.

The measureOn-time-in-full for the affected customers. Also: revenue protected, allocation overrides.

Procurement · resilience

Supplier disruption and alternate source

The decisionA supplier is late, cuts capacity, or is hit by a credit alert or a geopolitical event. Buying elsewhere costs a premium and needs a qualified source.

Who takes partBuyers, quality and the plant; the sourcing rules in the ERP; a procurement vendor's sourcing agent that may place the order itself.

What Aidecis doesMaps the affected materials and plants, compares approved alternates on lead time, price and qualification status, asks quality and procurement to approve, and limits the purchase-order change to the approved scope (enforced control). An order a vendor's agent places directly is read back and checked against its cap and the approved-supplier list (observed control).

How far it can goLevel 1 to 2. Procurement is where vendors' agents arrive first, so this is often where observed control starts.

The measureDays of cover protected. Also: premium purchase cost, time to qualify an alternate.

Quality · disposition

Quality hold, release or substitute

The decisionA QMS hold, a lab result or a nonconformance blocks a lot. Releasing, reworking or substituting it touches safety, compliance and customer orders.

Who takes partQuality assurance and the operations owners; the rules in the QMS and the laboratory system.

What Aidecis doesLinks the affected lots and orders to the customers downstream, blocks any release the rules do not allow, routes the disposition to the authorised quality owners and records the evidence behind every release or substitution.

How far it can goLevel 1. A release stays with a person; Aidecis makes it faster and fully evidenced, not automatic.

The measureTime to disposition. Also: affected volume, compliance deviations.

Manufacturing · recovery

Production rescheduling

The decisionA machine stops, a team is short or material is missing, and the schedule has to change without breaking the commitments downstream.

Who takes partProduction planners and the production owner; the scheduler in the APS or MES.

What Aidecis doesCompares feasible schedules and their downstream effects within the labour, maintenance, quality and customer constraints, asks the production owner to approve, and then writes the approved change to the MES or APS (enforced control).

How far it can goLevel 2 for routine changes inside a set envelope; larger changes stay with the production owner.

The measureTime to a recovered plan. Also: schedule adherence, changeover loss, overtime.

Logistics · routing

Logistics reroute or carrier choice

The decisionPort congestion, weather or a carrier failure disrupts a lane, and the freight has to move another way.

Who takes partLogistics planners; the routing rules in the TMS; a forwarder's or visibility platform's agent that proposes or books reroutes.

What Aidecis doesCompares route and carrier options on service, cost, emissions and contract terms, checks the approved-carrier list and the site caps, and sends exactly the approved booking change to the TMS (enforced control). A reroute a forwarder's agent books on its own is read back and flagged when it breaks the contract or the cap (observed control).

How far it can goLevel 2 within contracted carriers and a cost cap.

Procurement · spend control

Procurement price, quantity or PO exception

The decisionA supplier asks for a higher price, a larger quantity or an expedited purchase outside the agreed tolerance.

Who takes partBuyers and budget owners; the purchasing rules; a vendor's contract or spot-buy agent that may accept the change itself.

What Aidecis doesCompares the contract, budget, stock, criticality and alternatives, routes the approval by total commitment and delegated authority, and amends the order only within the approved scope (enforced control). Finance · reconciliation · later phase

Invoice variance, claim and close-out

The decisionAn invoice differs from the quote, the order, the receipt or the approved expedite.

Who takes partAccounts payable and the finance controllers; the matching rules in the ERP; an invoice-processing vendor's agent that may release payments.

What Aidecis doesMatches the invoice against the approved decision and what actually executed, sends differences outside the tolerance to finance, prepares the evidence for a claim or chargeback, and closes the case only after reconciliation. A payment an agent releases on its own is checked against the tolerance (observed control).

How far it can goLevel 3 is realistic early for small differences inside the tolerance: the check rests on facts the decision record already holds.

The measureLeakage recovered per month. Also: claim success, days to close.

Maintenance · asset failure

Unplanned failure: run reduced or stop to repair

The decisionA priority-1 notification on a critical asset, such as a seal leak on a refinery feed pump, with the spare part days away.

Who takes partThe site owner, maintenance and operations; the maintenance system and its work-order rules.

What Aidecis doesCompares running at a reduced rate, stopping to repair and switching to standby on lost throughput, margin, safety, permits and spare-part lead time, routes the choice to the site owner within the stop-duration envelope, and schedules the work order and the rate plan only after approval.

How far it can goLevel 1. With safety at stake a person decides; it starts as a shadow brief with a design partner.

Trading · decision chain

Buy a cargo, and the decisions it sets in motion

The decisionA trader sees a spread to buy an extra cargo against a monthly position limit. The purchase will need tank space, transport and an accrual downstream.

Who takes partThe trader and the head of trading; the terminal and logistics schedulers; the trading system and its limit checks.

What Aidecis doesChecks the commitment against the position limit and routes it to the head of trading. Once it is approved, opens the downstream decisions for tank allocation and barge nomination, each with its own owner, in one chain.

How far it can goLevel 1 for the trade itself; each downstream step can move up once it holds on the record.
