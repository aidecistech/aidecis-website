01

Logistics · reference dossier

Expedite freight after disruption

Trigger / contextLate ETA, line-stoppage risk, order value at risk, carrier quote and capacity.

Aidecis actionCompare expedite options; evaluate total disruption cost and delegated authority; route to logistics and finance approvers; issue a scoped TMS booking grant; reconcile the invoice and avoided stoppage.

Value evidenceDecision time; avoided downtime; premium spend; invoice variance; SLA compliance.

02

Inventory · allocation

Inventory reallocation

Trigger / contextShortage at one site, surplus at another, demand, shelf life, transit and transfer cost.

Aidecis actionRank recipient sites by service risk; protect minimum stock and reserved orders; route transfer by value and impact; create a WMS or ERP transfer only after approval.

Value evidenceFill rate; stockout hours; transfer cost; excess inventory; policy exceptions.

03

Planning · allocation

Demand surge allocation

Trigger / contextA demand signal or large order exceeds available-to-promise supply.

Aidecis actionCompare allocation options using customer commitments, margin, strategic rules and fairness; capture who approved any promise change; update planning or ERP after grant.

Value evidenceOn-time-in-full; revenue protected; promise changes; allocation overrides.

04

Procurement · resilience

Supplier disruption and alternate source

Trigger / contextSupplier delay, capacity cut, credit alert or geopolitical disruption.

Aidecis actionMap impacted materials and plants; compare approved alternates, lead times and qualification status; require quality and procurement approval; issue a narrowly scoped PO change.

Value evidenceDays of cover; production protected; premium purchase cost; alternate qualification time.

05

Quality · disposition

Quality hold, release or substitute

Trigger / contextQMS hold, lab result, nonconformance or product shortage.

Aidecis actionJoin affected lots, orders and downstream customers; block unsafe release; route disposition to authorized quality and operations roles; document substitution or release evidence.

Value evidenceTime to disposition; affected volume; compliance deviation; scrap and service impact.

06

Manufacturing · recovery

Production rescheduling

Trigger / contextMachine downtime, labor absence, material constraint, constrained line or urgent order.

Aidecis actionCompare feasible schedules and downstream effects; respect labor, maintenance, quality and customer constraints; require production-owner approval; write schedule changes to MES or APS.

Value evidenceSchedule adherence; changeover loss; recovery time; overtime; customer impact.

07

Logistics · routing

Logistics reroute or carrier choice

Trigger / contextLane disruption, port congestion, weather, carrier capacity or service failure.

Aidecis actionCompare route and carrier service, cost, emissions or contractual limits; check approved-carrier and site caps; approve, then issue the exact booking change to TMS.

Value evidenceTransit time; premium cost; service reliability; reroute delay; carrier compliance.

08

Customer service · prioritization

Customer promise and backorder priority

Trigger / contextDemand exceeds supply or a high-priority order risks missing its need date.

Aidecis actionShow impacted customers and order dependencies; apply the client priority policy; propose promise, split or substitution options; require authorized commercial or operations approval.

Value evidenceOTIF; promise accuracy; revenue protected; fairness and override rate.

09

Procurement · spend control

Procurement price, quantity or PO exception

Trigger / contextSupplier asks for a price change, quantity overage or expedited purchase outside tolerance.

Aidecis actionCompare contract, budget, inventory, criticality and substitute options; route by total commitment and delegated authority; create a PO amendment only within approved scope.

Value evidencePrice variance; maverick spend; approval time; supply continuity.

10

Finance · reconciliation

Invoice variance, claim and close-out

Trigger / contextInvoice differs from quote, purchase order, receipt or approved expedite.

Aidecis actionThree-way match against the dossier grant and actual execution; route tolerance breaches to finance; prepare supplier-claim or chargeback evidence; close only after reconciliation.

Value evidenceLeakage recovered; claim success; days to close; false disputes; unapproved spend.
