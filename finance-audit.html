For finance and audit

The authority matrix, enforced and proven.

Your delegation of authority, run as code. Aidecis checks every consequential decision and every write against the authority you signed, whoever or whatever made it, and keeps one record you can verify independently.

Not a trust exercise after the fact: the rights are executable before the action runs, and the record is yours to export and check without Aidecis.

What you get

Control that proves itself.

The four things finance, risk and internal audit ask of a young vendor, built into the product rather than the pitch.

  • Executable decision rights

    Your delegation of authority as code: who or what may take each decision, within which limit. A write runs only with a single-use grant that matches what was approved.

  • Every write checked

    Observed control reads the writes made in SAP directly, matches each against its registered role and limit, and flags a breach within minutes, including your own algorithms and other vendors' agents.

  • One record across vendors

    People, your algorithms, your vendors' agents and ours in one hash-chained record, each decision linked to its outcome.

  • Independent verification

    An open-source ledger verifier, so you or your auditor can check an export against the hash chain without Aidecis.

Mapped to your duties

Documentation your procurement, security and risk teams can screen.

The vendor-trust pack maps our security, privacy and AI-governance documentation to ISO/IEC 42001 and to your EU AI Act deployer duties, with our prices and an architecture walkthrough. ISO/IEC 42001 is mapped to, not certified; a penetration test is scheduled, and its status is stated where you rely on it.

Nothing is written to your systems.
