Already run a decision platform?

Govern what the platform cannot see.

Where you already run a planning or decision platform, Aidecis does not compete for its decision logic. It takes the platform in as one actor in the chain, like a planner or a buyer, and governs everything around it.

The platform governs what it decides itself. Aidecis governs the steps in other systems and teams, the authority behind every action, the writes made outside the platform, and the outcome of the whole chain.

Three ways in ↓

What Aidecis governs

The platform becomes one actor in the chain.

Its recommendations and its agents' actions become steps in the chain, each checked against the same authority as a person's or a buyer's.

  • Across other systems and teams

    The steps the platform does not reach, in SAP, the TMS, the WMS and the teams around them.

  • The authority behind every action

    One delegation-of-authority model for people, your agents, the platform's agents and ours.

  • Writes made outside the platform

    Every write checked against that authority, including the platform's own agent under its service user.

  • The outcome of the whole chain

    One record across vendors, linked to what the whole decision led to, not just the platform's step.

Three ways in, light to strong

Start read-only. Deepen on evidence.

  • 1 · Observed controlSAP change documents read and every write checked against the authority matrix, including the platform's agent. No integration with the platform.
  • 2 · Authority checkThe platform's agent asks Aidecis before it acts: allowed, needs approval or refused, in seconds, recorded in the same ledger.
  • 3 · The gateOnly the Aidecis gateway holds the write credential agents use; one single-use grant per action.

Who owns this

Sell to the chain and the authority, not the platform.

Aidecis is bought by whoever owns the chain outcome and the authority: the COO or CSCO for the chain, the CFO, controller and internal audit for the authority matrix. Not the platform's owner. The platform vendor can be a partner, because governance across systems helps it close deals. The moat is neutrality across vendors and your ownership of the authority matrix.

The entry question

Which decisions ran without an owner?

Enter with the decision replay assessment, read-only. It answers a sharper question in these accounts: which decisions and writes in the last six months crossed teams and systems without anyone holding the whole chain, and which fell outside the signed authority, including the platform's own agent.
