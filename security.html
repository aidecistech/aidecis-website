Security and data

How Aidecis protects your data and your authority

This page is for procurement, security and risk teams. It sets out how Aidecis is built, which data it reads and writes, where that data is hosted, who processes it, and what you keep if you leave. You can read it without a sales call.

Aidecis is the decision intelligence and control layer for consequential supply-chain decisions. It writes to your systems only through a gateway that runs in your environment, and only when your policy allows the action.

Read the architecture ↓

Architecture

Four parts, one boundary.

Your systems stay the systems of record. The gateway in your environment calls out to the Aidecis control plane; nothing connects into your network.

Your sideAidecis side, in the EU
  • Control plane

    Holds your policies, delegation of authority, approval routing and the decision record. It is one shared platform for all clients; each client is a separate tenant, and row-level security in the database scopes every request to that one tenant. The policy kernel is deterministic code. No AI decides what is allowed.

  • Execution gateway, on your side

    Software that runs on a virtual machine or container in your environment. It runs an action only with a signed, single-use grant bound to the exact route, object, action, amount, target and policy version. A tampered, expired, replayed or broadened grant does not execute, and a retry cannot create a second action.

  • Model gateway, hosted in the EU

    Language models draft briefs and explain flags. They run behind one model gateway on EU-owned infrastructure. It keeps no prompts or answers beyond the request, keeps tenants apart, holds no credentials and has no network path beyond its approved route. A model never sets an amount, approves or writes.

  • Hash-chained decision record

    Every step, review, approval, grant, execution and outcome is written to an append-only record in which each event carries the hash of the one before. Corrections are new events. A changed, removed or reordered event breaks the chain.

Fail-safe by default: when policy, identity, evidence or the gateway cannot be trusted, or the control plane cannot be reached, consequential writes hold. Your own systems keep running and your people decide as before.

Data flows

What Aidecis reads, writes and shows a model.

Access is declared per decision type in a data contract, and it grows in stages that you approve: files, read-only, live evidence, then a write route. The first slice of every pilot is read-only.

  • ReadOnly what the data contract declares

    Approved sources and fields, at the freshness the contract sets. Sensitive fields are excluded by default. Every fact keeps its source system, source record and time, so you can see where it came from.

  • WriteOnly through the gateway

    Only approved actions, on routes certified one object and one operation at a time, never a whole system. Each write carries an idempotency key and its result is reconciled into the decision record.

  • ModelMinimal fields, nothing kept

    A model receives only the fields a step needs, without your tenant identity or any credentials. Source text is treated as data, never as instructions. Nothing is stored after the request, and your data is never used to train or fine-tune a model unless you explicitly opt in.

Your administrator consents to scopes, purposes, processors, regions, model providers and any learning participation. Retention, deletion, export and legal-hold rules are set per data class and per contract. Connector access is reviewed on personnel change, incident, contract end and at each recertification.

Deployment options

The gateway always runs with you. You choose where the rest runs.

Aidecis supplies software only. Whichever option you choose, the policy kernel, evidence rules and containment rules are identical.

  • DefaultAidecis-hosted

    The control plane and the model gateway are hosted by Aidecis in the EU, with an open-weight model on EU-owned infrastructure. No third-party AI vendor is in the path. Nothing extra for you to host.

  • Inside your boundaryClient-hosted model

    The model runs on a host that you provide and manage, connected to the gateway. No model data leaves your network.

  • Only if you opt inExternal AI provider

    An external provider in an EU region, under contractual no-training terms, receiving minimal fields only. Used only when you explicitly choose it.

  • Priced separatelyDedicated control plane

    The control plane for your organisation only, on EU-owned infrastructure chosen with you or in your own environment, operated by Aidecis.

Aidecis can also run rules-led, with no language model at all.

Control coverage

What Aidecis enforces, and what it observes.

Enforced before execution through the Aidecis gateway; observed and reconciled after execution for registered actors outside it.

  • Before the actionEnforced control

    For actions that run through the Aidecis gateway. Policy and authority are checked before the action runs. Without a valid grant, the action does not execute.

  • After the actionObserved control

    For registered actors that write in their own systems with their own credentials, such as a vendor's agent or your own algorithm. Aidecis reads their writes from the systems' change records, checks them against the actor's role and limits, reconciles them into the decision record and flags a breach within the agreed time.

Observed control never prevents an action; it shows it. An observed decision is never labelled as authorised before the fact. You choose which actors move from observed to enforced.

Security practice

Controls you can test.

  • Identity and segregation of duties

    Single sign-on with your identity provider over OIDC, multi-factor authentication through that provider, and SCIM provisioning. Nobody approves their own proposal, and the sponsor of an agent cannot approve that agent's proposals.

  • Agents with limited keys

    Each agent has its own key, stored only as a hash, limited to reading and proposing, and valid for at most 90 days. Switching off the agent or its sponsor stops the next request.

  • Containment of language models

    A model runs as a separate process with no credentials, no write tools and no network access beyond its approved endpoint. An instruction planted in an e-mail, a supplier message or an ERP text field cannot cause an action, because only the gateway writes, and only with a grant no model can create.

  • Engineering and operations

    Every change goes through a reviewed pull request. Dependencies, secrets, container images and code are scanned automatically on every change and every week. Backups run nightly, are encrypted off-site and are restore-tested.

Continuity and exit

Your governance does not depend on a young vendor.

You own your decisions, dossiers, run records, evaluation sets and accepted policies. Every subscription carries a continuity package.

  • Full export in open formats

    Your administrator exports everything your environment holds, in JSON and CSV, at any time and at the end of the contract, without asking Aidecis.

  • Open-source ledger verifier

    A verifier under the MIT licence checks an export against its hash chains and file hashes on your own machine, with no Aidecis system and no internet connection. A copy comes with every export.

  • Source-code escrow

    A contract term: the source code of the control kernel and the gateway is held by a named escrow agent and released on insolvency or end of support, with a guide to build and run it.

  • Exit assistance and deletion

    A contract term: an agreed number of days at the published day rate to move your policies and records to the platform you choose. Once you confirm you have your export, Aidecis deletes your environment's data.
