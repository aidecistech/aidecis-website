Buyer questions

The questions buyers ask, answered straight.

These are the questions an operations, IT or procurement team asks before a first meeting with Aidecis. Where the answer is no, or not yet, we say so.

Start with scope ↓

Scope

What Aidecis is for, and what it is not.

Aidecis governs consequential supply-chain decisions. It does not run the plant, plan the network or replace the ERP.

Does Aidecis improve OEE, downtime or throughput?

Not directly. Aidecis does not measure equipment effectiveness, schedule the line or control machines. SAP Digital Manufacturing, Siemens Opcenter, Celonis and industrial AI tools do that. Aidecis governs the decisions that follow: who may approve a reschedule, a quality release or an expedite, within which limit, and what the result was. Where a decision affects throughput, its outcome is linked to it in your own measure.

Can it find production bottlenecks and recommend schedule changes?

Not as a planning or scheduling engine. When your APS, your MES or a planner proposes a schedule change, Aidecis compares the feasible options against their downstream effects, asks the production owner to approve, and writes the approved change through its gateway. Production rescheduling is one of the decision families; the first focus is procurement and supply-chain decisions.

How does it work with MES, SCADA and machine data?

MES and quality systems can be sources of evidence and, through the gateway, targets for approved actions. Aidecis is not a SCADA layer and never controls a machine. Machine data enters only as evidence where a decision needs it, as agreed in the data contract for each project.

Does it replace SAP, our planning suite or our MES?

No. It sits on top of them. Your systems stay the systems of record, and Aidecis writes to them only through a gateway in your environment, when your policy allows the action.

SAP

How Aidecis meets your SAP environment.

The way in is read-only. Writes come later, one route at a time, and only after evidence.

Which SAP environment do you support?

Aidecis is built for SAP S/4HANA. Other SAP releases are checked per project. To start, the assessment uses a read-only extract: purchasing documents and their history, goods receipts and change documents, with stock, production demand and invoice items where a chain is replayed. No connection to a live system is needed.

What happens when we move from SAP ECC to S/4HANA, or to another ERP?

Your decision rules, authority and decision record stay. Aidecis sits above the ERP, so a migration changes the connection, not the governance. That matters now that mainstream maintenance for SAP ECC ends in 2027.

Can you show a real integration with our SAP?

Yes, on your own data. The decision replay assessment replays six months of your purchasing decisions against your policy and delegation of authority, read-only. The public demo runs on simulated systems and a fictional company, and is labelled as such.

How do writes into SAP work?

Only through the Aidecis gateway, which runs in your environment. Each write needs a signed, single-use grant that matches the approved action, and routes are certified one object and one operation at a time. The first gated write goes to your SAP test system, after a shadow run.

Proof and status

What is available now, and what is not yet.

We keep what is available, simulated, in pilot and proposed apart, so you can judge each claim on its own.

Can you show a live customer deployment with measured results?

Not yet. Aidecis is in its founding-client phase and has no published client results. We would rather say that than show a logo we cannot back. Founding clients get founding-client terms in exchange for real cases and, if the pilot succeeds, a reference. Every pilot is anchored to one hard measure agreed before it starts, such as disruption response time, premium freight spend or stockout hours.

Can you show a complete decision workflow, from problem to financial impact?

Yes. The demo follows a late delivery to the production order and the customer promise it puts at risk, weighs the options, finds the owner whose limit covers the decision, records the approval and the scoped action, and links the outcome. It runs on simulated systems. In a pilot the same chain runs on your own history, read-only first.

What is available at launch?

People, hybrid and bounded bot modes; enforced and observed control; the seven-step workflow from detection to recorded outcome; the decision replay assessment and evidence sprints; and the continuity package with open export, an open-source ledger verifier and escrow.

What is proposed, and not yet available?

Agent-to-agent chains, where one bot hands off to the next without a person in between. We offer them as a bounded pilot to founding clients, one chain at a time. No such chain runs in production today.

Control

How Aidecis governs people, bots and agents.

Two levels of control, and a strict gate before any decision moves from people to bots.

Can one agent hand off to another agent?

Only per chain, never by default. A chain qualifies when the process is closed, every agent in it acts through the enforced gateway, one owner is accountable for the whole chain, a cap limits the total value at risk, every step is reversible with a tested rollback, and the release conditions hold on your own replayed decisions. A breach at any step returns the whole chain to hybrid.

What happens when a vendor agent or our MRP writes straight into SAP?

Aidecis observes it. The write is detected from the change records, matched against the actor's registered role and limits, and flagged within minutes when it falls outside them. Observed control does not stop the action. You decide per decision type when an actor moves to enforced control, for example for every write above a set amount.

Do we have to change system permissions to start?

No. You start observed, with no change to write permissions, and measure how often writes fall outside role or limit. That evidence shows where enforcement is worth it.

Commercial

What it costs to start, and to run.

Published, indicative prices, excluding taxes. Implementation is always scoped with you first.

What does Aidecis cost?

A pilot or evidence sprint on one decision type costs €16,000 to €24,000 (10 to 15 engineer days at €1,600). The subscription is €45,000 per year, including the first site and two regions. Implementation is €1,600 per day, scoped separately. There is no per-user fee.

How do we build the business case?

Start from one decision type and one hard measure in your own terms. The calculator on the benefits page uses your own exposure, addressable share and reduction assumptions, net of review effort. It is a scenario estimate, not a savings forecast.

What if we stop?

Every subscription includes the continuity package: export in open formats, an open-source ledger verifier, escrow of the control kernel and gateway, and exit assistance at a published day rate.
