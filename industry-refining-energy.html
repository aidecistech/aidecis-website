Refining and energy

Refineries, terminals and energy-intensive sites run continuous assets close to their limits, and a pump failure, a crude slate change or a price spike forces decisions within hours. Those decisions are taken by people, by your own planning and scheduling optimisers and maintenance rules, and by the agents of trading desks, terminals and shipping brokers. Aidecis gives them one authority model and one decision record across rate, maintenance, cargoes and storage, each within its safety, margin and trading limits.

Current challenges

  • Unplanned failures force a choice between running at a reduced rate and stopping to repair.
  • Trading, tank and transport decisions depend on each other, but are taken in different systems.
  • Permits, emission limits and safety rules narrow every recovery option.

How Aidecis helps

  • Register the software that already decides, such as refinery planning and scheduling optimisers, maintenance planning rules, and terminal, broker or trading agents, with an owner, limits and a mode.
  • Enforced control: a rate cut, a maintenance deferral or a cargo decision sent through the Aidecis gateway runs only with a single-use grant from the owner whose limits fit; safety conflicts are held for review.
  • Observed control (in development): when a broker's or terminal's agent changes a nomination or a booking in its own system, Aidecis reads the change afterwards, checks it against that agent's role and limits, and flags it within minutes if it went outside them. It does not prevent the change.
  • Keep the decision chain visible, from a cargo or an outage to the tank, transport and customer decisions it sets in motion.
  • Automation stays low on the scale: agents and algorithms prepare and propose (autonomy level 1 to 2), and people decide. Process-safety decisions, management of change and trading beyond mandates never move to bot mode.
  • How far a decision may climb also depends on your maturity in that decision family. For basic or digital organisations we propose reviewed assistance and bounded pilots, not bot mode. Agent-to-agent chains are not offered at launch.

Potential benefits

  • Throughput and margin protected by faster, evidenced decisions on rate and repair.
  • Fewer demurrage costs and storage surprises downstream of trading decisions.
  • Clear responsibility for safety-relevant deviations and their management of change.

These are potential improvements to test with your team. We agree a baseline, measure the pilot results and avoid counting the same benefit twice.

Operating context

Process safety management, management of change, emission permits, and trading mandates and position limits.
